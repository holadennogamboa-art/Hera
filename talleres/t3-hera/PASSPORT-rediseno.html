WHAT THE PASSPORT CERTIFIES

01 · ORIGIN

What was shot.

The master comes from a real, human-directed shoot. Every version traces back to it.

02 · PROCESS

What the machine did.

Where AI extended, adapted or versioned the image, it is declared — in the file, not in a footnote.

03 · GEO-LOCK

The product, untouched.

Shape, proportion, engraving and stitching are locked to the real piece. Every version is checked against it, one by one.

04 · HAND

Who signed it off.

Skin, fabric and colour are retouched and approved by a person, against your own brand codes.

05 · RECORD

How to prove it.

Content Credentials travel inside the file. Anyone — a platform, a regulator, your legal team — can read them.
