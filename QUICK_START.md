## IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

### Post got 0 comments after 2 hours:

**Don't panic.** Instagram takes 2-4 hours to push content.

Action: Post another strong piece Wednesday. Move on.

### No growth after 1 week:

This is normal. Growth accelerates after post 5-6.

Action: Keep posting. Improve captions (add questions).

### Someone left hate comment:

Ignore it. Don't respond. Delete if it's spam.

Action: Keep moving. Haters gonna hate.

### You're exhausted:

Take 1 day off. That's it. Don't quit.

Action: Create 1-2 posts and batch them for the week. Less daily work.

## THE MENTAL GAME

### Things to know:

1. **First 2 weeks are hardest.** You're posting to almost nobody. This is normal. Stick with it.

2. **Month 1 is sacrifice. Month 2 is payoff.** By week 4, you'll feel momentum. By week 8, it compounds.

3. **Quality compounds.** One amazing post > 10 mediocre posts. Keep the bar high.

4. **Consistency beats perfection.** It's better to post something good every 4 days than to be perfect once a month.

5. **Your job isn't to go viral.** Your job is to build a community of people who get what you're making.
