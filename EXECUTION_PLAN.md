## PART 6: DETAILED CAPTION FORMULA

### The Structure (use for every post)

**Line 1: Hook (1 sentence)**
- Poetic, intriguing, or asks a question
- Examples:
  - "the frame is the portal."
  - "what do you see when you look?"
  - "every landscape is a portrait."

**Line 2-3: Expansion (2-3 sentences)**
- Provide context or storytelling
- Make it visual/emotional
- Don't explain literally (let it breathe)

**Line 4: Call-to-action (1 sentence, subtle)**
- Ask a question that invites thought
- NOT "follow for more" (please never)
- Examples:
  - "what world would you enter?"
  - "how do you interpret the space between subject and backdrop?"
  - "do you see fashion here, or art?"

**Line 5: Hashtags (secondary comment)**
- Post hashtags as first reply (better algorithm)
- 10-20 hashtags (not 30+, looks spammy)

## PART 8: COLLABORATION TYPES & EXECUTION

### Type 1: Cross-Tag (Easiest)

**What:** Post featuring their account + they share to stories
**ROI:** 10-30 new followers
**Effort:** Low
**Timeline:** 1 week

**Execution:**
1. Create post (carousel or reel) featuring them/their work
2. Tag them in post + caption
3. Send DM: "Posted something inspired by your work. Would love if you shared to stories."
4. Expect: 50-70% of requests result in share

---

### Type 2: Duet/Transition (Medium)

**What:** Reel that duets or transitions with their content
**ROI:** 20-50 new followers (if they share)
**Effort:** Medium
**Timeline:** 2 weeks

**Execution:**
1. Find their best reel
2. Create transition/duet version
3. Tag + DM: "Made this inspired by [their video]. Would love your take."
4. They repost = viral moment

---

### Type 3: Joint Content Creation (Hard)

**What:** Shoot new content together
**ROI:** 50-200 new followers (if they have good following)
**Effort:** High
**Timeline:** 4-6 weeks (planning + execution + editing)

**Execution:**
1. Pitch: "Want to collaborate on a shoot? I'm thinking [concept]..."
2. Plan: Location, theme, timeline
3. Shoot: 4-8 hours
4. Edit: 1-2 weeks
5. Post on both accounts (synchronized)
6. Each cross-promotes

**Best partnerships:** 2-3 of these by month 6 = 500+ followers each

## PART 10: RED FLAGS & PIVOTS

### Red Flag 1: Low engagement after Week 2

**Symptom:** <3% engagement rate, few comments

**Diagnosis:** Content quality or caption quality issue

**Action:**
1. Review captions: Are they asking questions? Are they vague/unclear?
2. Review content: Is it as strong as you thought?
3. Pivot: Improve captions (add questions), improve content (shoot more/edit better)

---

### Red Flag 2: Followers but no community engagement

**Symptom:** 500 followers, but only 3 comments per post

**Diagnosis:** Wrong audience or content not sparking thought

**Action:**
1. Check who's following: Are they visual creatives or random?
2. Adjust captions: Make them more thought-provoking
3. Adjust content: Are you making things you'd want to engage with?

---

### Red Flag 3: Stuck at 2k followers (month 2)

**Symptom:** 1st month hit 1k, but growing at 50/week in month 2

**Diagnosis:** Algorithm plateau or saturation

**Action:**
1. Increase community engagement (more comments on others' posts)
2. Try new content format (if was carousel, try reel; if single, try carousel)
3. Ask for collabs (network effect is only way through plateau)

---

### Red Flag 4: Burnout (you don't want to create anymore)

**Symptom:** Posting feels like work, not excited anymore

**Diagnosis:** Creating too much or not aligned with vision

**Action:**
1. Take 3-day break (seriously)
2. Create only 1 post/week for 2 weeks (reduce pressure)
3. Remember why: Are you building something you believe in? If no → pivot
4. Batch create: Do all editing in 1 day (better than daily)

## APPENDIX: CRISIS MANAGEMENT

### Scenario 1: Content Flopped Harder Than Expected

**If your best content gets <2% engagement:**

1. Don't panic
2. Post again ASAP (don't let algorithm think you're dead)
3. Analyze: What's different about high-engagement posts?
4. Adjust captions/content
5. Move forward

### Scenario 2: Account Got Shadow-Banned

**Signs:** Reach dropped 50%+, engagement dying, less reach per follower

**Action:**
1. Stop all engagement for 48 hours (let it reset)
2. Post 1 strong post
3. Wait 1 week
4. If still shadow-banned: Might need to start over (Instagram issue, not you)

### Scenario 3: Major Creator Ignored Your DM

**Don't:**
- Send follow-up DMs
- Comment aggressively
- Get upset

**Do:**
- Move on
- Keep engaging with their content naturally
- Try again in 3 months when you have more followers
