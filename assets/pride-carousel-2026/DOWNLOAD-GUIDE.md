# 📥 How to Download the Carousel

## Option 1: Screenshot from Browser (Fastest)

### Desktop:
1. Open `carousel.html` in your browser
2. For each slide:
   - Take a screenshot (Cmd+Shift+4 on Mac / Win+Shift+S on Windows)
   - Save as `slide-1.png`, `slide-2.png`, etc.
3. You now have 6 PNG files

### Mobile:
1. Open the carousel on your phone
2. For each slide:
   - Take a screenshot (volume down + power button)
   - It saves to your Photos
3. Share via Instagram

## Option 2: Generate Images Automatically (Better Quality)

## Option 3: Using Canva (Most Customizable)

1. Go to **canva.com**
2. Create new design → 1080×1920 px
3. Copy text from each slide
4. Download as PNG

See `README.md` for exact colors and styling.
