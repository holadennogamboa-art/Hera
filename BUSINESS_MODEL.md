# EYEHERA — BUSINESS MODEL & REVENUE PLAN
## Long-term Monetization Strategy

---

## PHASE 1: FOUNDATION (Month 0-3)
### Goal: Establish credibility and audience
### Revenue: $0

**Activities:**
- Build to 10k followers
- Establish visual language
- Create 15-20 pieces of original content
- Secure 2-3 partnership conversations

**Investment:**
- Time: 10 hours/week
- Money: $0-2k (optional: editor, higher quality production)
- ROI: Brand building (future revenue)

---

## PHASE 2: MONETIZATION (Month 4-6)
### Goal: Begin revenue generation
### Revenue Target: $5-15k

### Revenue Stream 1: Sponsored Posts (Brand Partnerships)

**How it works:**
- Brands pay you to feature their product in posts/reels
- You create authentic content (not salesy)
- Brands care about: audience alignment, engagement rate

**Pricing model:**
- At 10k followers: $500-2k per post
- At 25k followers: $2k-5k per post
- At 50k+ followers: $5k-15k+ per post

**Target brands:**
- Conceptual fashion brands (Yohji Yamamoto, CCP, Archival)
- Camera/equipment brands (Sony, Canon, DJI)
- Luxury streetwear (STÜSSY, Needles)
- Subscription boxes (Patreon-adjacent)

**Expected:** 2-3 brand deals per month = $2-6k/month

---

### Revenue Stream 2: Photography Licensing

**How it works:**
- Magazines, brands, agencies license your images
- You maintain ownership; they pay for usage rights

**Pricing:**
- Magazine/editorial use: $500-2k per image
- Brand use (social): $1-3k per image
- Commercial (billboard/print): $5-10k+ per image

**Platforms:**
- Direct outreach (your network)
- Licensing platforms (Shutterstock, Getty Images)
- Licensing agents (find agent who takes 20-30% commission)

**Expected:** 1-2 images licensed per month = $1-3k/month

---

### Revenue Stream 3: Consulting & Art Direction

**How it works:**
- Other creators/brands hire you as creative consultant
- You advise on visual direction, concept, aesthetic

**Pricing:**
- Per-project consultation: $2-5k
- Full project art direction: $5-15k
- Retainer (monthly): $1-5k/month

**Clients:**
- Emerging fashion photographers
- Small agencies
- Indie brands
- Content creators

**Expected:** 1 project per quarter = $2-5k/month

---

### Revenue Stream 4: Educational Content (Patreon/Membership)

**How it works:**
- Exclusive content (BTS videos, process, philosophy)
- Monthly subscribers pay for access

**Pricing:**
- Tier 1: $5/month (exclusive stories, process videos)
- Tier 2: $15/month (monthly 1:1 feedback on their work)
- Tier 3: $50/month (direct access, project collaboration)

**Content:**
- Weekly behind-the-scenes videos
- Process breakdowns (how you create a reel)
- Photography critiques
- Monthly live Q&A

**Expected:** 50-100 subscribers at avg $12 = $600-1200/month

---

### Revenue Stream 5: Merchandise (Long-term)

**How it works:**
- Create products featuring your visual language
- T-shirts, hoodies, prints, books, coffee table book

**Pricing:**
- T-shirt: $30-50 (margin: 40-50%)
- Print: $50-200 (margin: 60-70%)
- Coffee table book: $80-150 (margin: 30-40%)
- Limited edition: $200-500

**Expected:** $2-10k/month at scale (month 9+)

## PRICING NEGOTIATION GUIDE

### Brands Ask: "What's your rate?"

**NEVER say:** "Whatever you want to pay"

**ALWAYS have:** A tiered pricing structure

**Standard rates (use as baseline):**

| Followers | Sponsored Post | Sponsored Reel | Monthly Retainer |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5k-10k | $300-800 | $500-1.5k | $1-2k |
| 10k-25k | $800-2k | $1.5k-4k | $2-5k |
| 25k-50k | $2k-5k | $4k-10k | $5-10k |
| 50k-100k | $5k-12k | $10k-25k | $10-20k |
| 100k+ | $12k+ | $25k+ | $20k+ |

### Negotiation Tactics

**If brand offers low:**
"I appreciate the offer. My rate for this audience size is typically [X]. Can we meet at [70% of your ask]?"

**If brand is perfect fit but low budget:**
"Budget is an issue, but I love the brand. Could we do a smaller deal now and revisit in 3 months?"

**If brand wants exclusivity:**
"Exclusivity changes the rate. That would be [2-3x normal rate]."

**If brand wants usage rights:**
"Standard rate is for 30-day Instagram use. Extended rights (reprints, print media) would be [additional cost]."

## LICENSING STRATEGY

### Direct Licensing (Highest ROI)

**How it works:**
- Magazine reaches out: "Can we use Image X for our cover?"
- You negotiate terms (price, usage rights, duration)
- You retain ownership; they get limited rights

**Steps:**
1. Build relationships with magazine editors
2. Share work regularly
3. When they ask: Negotiate
4. Standard magazine rate: $500-2k per image

### Agent Representation (Easier but Lower %)

**How it works:**
- Agent finds licensing deals
- Agent takes 20-30% commission
- You handle creative; agent handles business

**When to get agent:**
- Month 6+ (when you have portfolio)
- Only if getting 3+ licensing inquiries/month

**Finding an agent:**
- Search "photography licensing agent" + your niche
- Contact agents at Getty Images, Shutterstock
- Network with photographers who have agents

## RISK MITIGATION

### Revenue Concentration Risk

**Problem:** Relying too much on one brand or stream

**Solution:**
- Diversify across 5+ revenue streams
- No single partnership > 20% of revenue
- Monthly targets per stream (not just total)

### Platform Risk

**Problem:** Instagram algorithm changes hurt reach

**Solution:**
- Build email list (link in bio, lead magnet)
- Cross-post to TikTok, YouTube
- Diversify: Website, newsletter, Patreon

### Market Risk

**Problem:** Fashion niche becomes saturated

**Solution:**
- Expand to adjacent niches (art direction, film, design)
- Build course/educational business (less competition)
- Move upmarket (agencies, film studios) vs. consumer

**This business model works IF:**
